---
title: Roadmap
description: High-level product direction for Basic.
---

Public direction (not commitments or ETAs):

- **SDKs and clients**: Broader first-party support (for example Python, native iOS/Android) beyond React, Next.js, Expo, and REST.
- **Collaboration**: Richer real-time and multiplayer-style patterns on top of sync.
- **Schema and types**: Stronger TypeScript and codegen from schema definitions.
- **History and undo**: Versioning primitives for user-visible undo/redo style flows.
- **Sync**: Smarter partial sync so large accounts do not need the full corpus on every device.
- **Deployment**: Self-hosted and bring-your-own-infrastructure options for datastores.

For what ships when, follow [Twitter / X](https://twitter.com/basic_db) and [Discord](https://discord.gg/PuWD3zhsCk)—this list is intentionally high level.
